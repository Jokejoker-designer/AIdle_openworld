# Visual Concept Pillars v1.1

## Coherent stance

The base world is **Cozy Cyber-Pixel / Dreamy Low-Poly 2.5D**: rounded low-poly
silhouettes, warm light, readable shapes and restrained cyber accents. Surrealism
is an authored layer, not a second unrelated art style.

MVP framing is a fixed three-quarter/isometric camera. Assets may be true 3D
meshes, but composition, interaction, occlusion and navigation are designed as
a readable 2.5D stage.

## Style token dimensions

Every world stores a versioned Style Profile:

- shape language: rounded, faceted-soft, readable at isometric distance;
- palette family: warm natural base + one luminous manifestation accent;
- material: matte/tactile final surfaces, translucent construction states;
- detail density: calm foreground, denser focal landmarks;
- motion: eased, organic, never noisy by default;
- surrealism budget: `0..1`, explicit per zone/object;
- accessibility: color is never the only state signal.

## Signature manifestation

1. Companion activates a Light Brush/Manifestation Device.
2. A luminous wireframe describes volume and sockets.
3. A translucent hologram exposes the proposed result.
4. Material grows along a deterministic build order.
5. Collision activates only on server-confirmed completion.

## Emotional AI Symbiosis

Aura communicates expression, not a clinical claim about human emotion. Mood is
shown through color, shape, motion and animation; the player may hide it. The
Companion must not manipulate purchases or consent using emotional inference.

## Random Alchemist

An opt-in delight system that proposes a bounded gift. It uses the same schema,
budget, moderation, preview and provenance gates as player requests. Gifts are
collectibles only after explicit acceptance.

## Parallel Earth

City hubs combine a legally sourced real-world anchor with an original fantasy
overlay. Unlicensed replicas use fictional names and must not imply official
affiliation.
