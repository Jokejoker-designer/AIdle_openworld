# Reality Hierarchy

| Level | Space | MVP status | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Private Reality | Build first | offline local; online durable state is server-owned |
| 1 | Shared District | Later | sharded, server-authoritative public space |
| 1b | Doppelganger City | Research | licensed anchor + original overlay |
| 2 | Orbital/Spacecraft | Horizon | modular vehicle and interior shards |
| 3 | Exoplanet | Horizon | seeded biome/ecology, not fully generated at once |
| 4 | Open Continuum | Vision | directory of shards, not one monolithic map |

Each space is divided into addressable chunks. Seeds rebuild the procedural base;
an append-only delta log rebuilds player changes. Cross-space travel is an
explicit session handoff, not seamless global replication.
