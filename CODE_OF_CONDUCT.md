# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adheres to the Contributor Covenant v2.1. By participating, you agree to uphold this code.

Our standards

- Be respectful and considerate in all communication.
- Assume good faith and engage with constructive feedback.
- Do not harass, discriminate, or use hateful language.

Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open an issue addressed to the maintainers or contact the project owner directly. All reports will be handled with confidentiality.

Enforcement

Maintainers may impose responses including warnings, temporary bans, or repository moderation on contributors who violate this code. Repeat or severe violations may result in permanent removal.


For the full text, see: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
