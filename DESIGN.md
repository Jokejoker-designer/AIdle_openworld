# AIdle Openworld 2.5D Design System

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Cozy Cyber-Pixel / Dreamy Low-Poly 2.5D. Warm, tactile and playful; technology
appears as gentle light, never as a cold dashboard pasted over the world.

## 2. Color

- Dawn sky `#9ED7E5`; warm ground `#C9B98A`; leaf `#72A96B`.
- Ink `#263238`; cream light `#FFF1C7`; manifestation cyan `#62E6FF`.
- Companion warmth `#FFB86B`; danger/rejection `#D85C5C`.
- One luminous accent dominates a scene; color never carries state alone.

## 3. Typography

Rounded humanist sans for UI; compact geometric display for world titles; mono
only for prompt/schema/debug surfaces. Vietnamese diacritics are mandatory.

## 4. Spacing & Grid

8px UI rhythm. World placement uses a layered 2.5D grid with elevation sockets.
Large touch targets and safe margins preserve play on handheld-sized screens.

## 5. Layout & Composition

Fixed three-quarter/isometric camera. Clear silhouettes, calm negative space and
foreground fade prevent occlusion. Prompt interaction occupies a small companion
bubble, not a permanent full-screen editor.

## 6. Components

Companion bubble, Prompt Composer, Proposal Card, Cost/Permission Receipt,
Manifestation Timeline, Undo Chip, World Map and Social Visit Card share tokens.

## 7. Motion & Interaction

Wireframe -> hologram -> material growth uses eased 8–15 second choreography.
Reduced-motion mode shortens transitions while preserving explicit stage labels.

## 8. Voice & Brand

Warm, curious and honest. The Companion says what it understood, what it cannot
do and what will cost resources. It asks before consequential changes.

## 9. Anti-patterns

- No dense cyberpunk neon soup, photoreal asset beside toy-like low-poly art, or
  arbitrary purple gradients.
- No instant pop-in for manifested objects.
- No tiny unreadable pixel fonts, camera spin or hidden collision changes.
- No copied Stardew Valley characters, tiles, UI, brand or exact composition.
- No emotional language designed to pressure purchases or consent.
