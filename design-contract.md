# AIdle 2.5D Reference Design Contract

## Goal and audience

Create a relaxing AI-native 2.5D world for creative players and friends. The
first artifact must prove prompt-to-world manifestation in a Private Reality.

## Evidence

| Signal | Status | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Cozy farming-game familiarity | provided | readable rhythm and emotional warmth |
| Play Together social world | provided | approachable visits and lightweight social UX |
| Cyber/low-poly/surreal concepts | provided | original AIdle visual vocabulary |
| Exact target hardware | unknown | performance budget must be measured later |

## Keep / Change / Do not copy

| Keep | Change | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|
| cozy pacing, readable scale, social warmth | true 2.5D low-poly stage, AI light construction | characters, tiles, logos, UI or exact layouts from reference games |
| surreal possibility | constrain by zone/style budget | copyrighted landmarks/brands without rights |
| visible generative magic | use deterministic stages and receipts | generic AI neon overload |

## Final stance

A warm diorama-like world whose physical surfaces feel handcrafted while AI
construction appears as cyan light drawn by a living Companion. Surreal elements
are rare focal events, making imagination visible without destroying coherence.

## Risks and unknowns

Camera occlusion, mobile/PC target, asset polygon budget, landmark licensing and
Companion animation scope require prototypes and owner decisions.

## Quality gate

- A screenshot reads as 2.5D AIdle without a prompt overlay.
- Preview and committed state are visually distinguishable.
- House, player and Companion silhouettes remain readable behind foregrounds.
- Reduced motion and non-color state indicators work.
- No referenced product is literally reproduced.
