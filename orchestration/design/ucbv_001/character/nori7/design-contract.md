# Design contract — U2 Nori-7 visual silhouette (+ C0 Directive 83)

## Goal

Produce a reusable **character visual silhouette package** for Nori-7 so that
turnaround art, material slotting, and production mesh work execute without
re-deciding identity, palette authority, or silhouette family. **C0** locks
cream reconciliation, nearly-white remediation, and animation contract intent
for C1 production.

**Target artifact:** visual_spec + silhouette/proportion sheets + modular
body/outfit material definition + C0 animation/cream locks (design/metadata).  
**Audience:** C1 production (Blender Bridge), C2 runtime, Red/QA/Purple/Human.  
**Not target (C0):** production GLB binary, runtime scripts, block modules.

## Final design stance

One warm, rounded, **2-heads-tall teardrop gardener robot** with mechanical
sprout crown and rear water tank, painted in **canonical bible cream**
(`#fdf3e2`/`#efe0c8`) with **readable darker leaf joints/panels and face**,
mapped only to live `MAT_*`, never flat near-white, DNA cream alias-only.
Animation production is real keyed GLB actions under Tier3 names + UCBV build
extension. Readable as black mass from front/side/back/¾ at dual resolutions,
modular only through recipe attachments.
