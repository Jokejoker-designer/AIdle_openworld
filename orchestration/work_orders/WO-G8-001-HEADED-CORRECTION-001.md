## Required playable result

### Physical Starter Realm

The headed scene must visibly contain, at minimum:

- a small cozy starter house or clearly readable house site;
- farm plots with differentiated soil/crop markers;
- a path connecting player spawn, house and farm;
- at least three environmental prop groups such as trees, fence, lamps, pond,
  stones or flowers;
- contrasting ground/sky, soft directional lighting and readable depth;
- recognizable player and Companion silhouettes with the Companion aura.

Use art-style palette switching without reducing the world to a single flat
color. Surrealism may alter palette/forms, but landmarks remain readable.

### Headed interaction surface

- HUD remains readable and unclipped at 1280x720 and 868x517.
- Controls and current edition/art/space are visible.
- `E` or an explicit button opens the text-only Companion panel.
- Free edition exposes visible `Send Snapshot to Desktop AI` and
  `Import Decision` actions through the existing Desktop Bridge, with manual
  consent. Paid fixture mode must not request/store a client secret.
- A deterministic local onboarding/demo intent can exercise the real safe
  pipeline without pretending to be a live LLM.
- A build proposal is pending and visibly previewed before confirmation.
- Confirm and Cancel are separate visible actions. Cancel leaves no collision
  or orphan. Confirm may only reach existing World Commit handoff/local
  permitted path; UI must not mutate durable state directly.
