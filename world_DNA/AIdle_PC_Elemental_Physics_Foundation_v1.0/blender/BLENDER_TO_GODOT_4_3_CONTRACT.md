# Blender → Godot 4.3

Blender exports mesh/LOD, armatures/actions, material slots, socket empties,
AIdle custom metadata, collision/fluid/reaction hints, state variants, GLB and
artifact manifest.

Godot creates StaticBody3D/RigidBody3D/AnimatableBody3D, CollisionShape3D,
Area3D sensors, reaction state, Simulation LOD, ownership and save IDs.
